Description- The Mini Spectrum Analyzer is a compact, handheld RF measurement instrument designed to visualize radio frequency signals over a selected frequency range. It combines a low-noise RF front end, a frequency synthesizer, a high-linearity mixer, a logarithmic detector, and a microcontroller-based processing system to provide real-time spectrum analysis on an integrated SPI display. The RF input is conditioned and mixed with a programmable local oscillator generated by the ADF4351 frequency synthesizer, producing an intermediate frequency that is processed by the ADL5801 mixer and detected by the AD8313 logarithmic detector. The STM32G031K8T6 microcontroller controls the frequency sweep, acquires detector output through its ADC, processes the sampled data, and renders the spectrum on the display. User interaction is provided through tactile push buttons for navigation and analyzer control. The hardware incorporates USB-C power input, overcurrent protection, regulated 3.3 V and 5 V power rails, decoupling networks, the project demonstrates practical RF circuit design, mixed-signal PCB layout, power management, embedded firmware integration, and user interface implementation, making it a comprehensive educational and portfolio-ready hardware development project.
